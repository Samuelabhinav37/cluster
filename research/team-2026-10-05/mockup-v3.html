Help

Short answers to the questions people ask first.

What does Cluster read?
Senders, subjects, dates, labels and sender-check headers. Never message bodies.
Does it work with Chrome closed?
Gmail filters label new mail at any time. Moving mail out of the inbox after a time limit happens while Chrome is open.
How do I undo something?
Use Undo on the message that appears, or open Activity. Trash can be undone for 30 days.
