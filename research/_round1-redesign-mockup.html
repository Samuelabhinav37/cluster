Paid subscriptions and trials

AAppleRenews

How Cluster spots these. From the sender and subject only:
  1. "Your free trial ends" in a subject means Trial ending.
  2. "Renews on" or "auto-renewal" means Renews.
  3. Mail from a known paid service like Netflix, if it isn't an ad.
It can't see prices, so it may miss some.
