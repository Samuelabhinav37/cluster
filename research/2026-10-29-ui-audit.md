# UI audit (2026-10-29)

Walked every place in the preview (`npm run preview:ui`, fixture mailbox, 1456×819 window,
Details panel open as it opens by default). Each finding is tagged with the problem type from
`2026-10-29-options-inventory.md`: **T1** one action many places, **T2** several systems for one
job, **T3** wrong place, **T4** unreadable, **T5** too deep, **T6** big one-tap action, plus
**B** for broken layout and **N** for numbers that disagree.

## Broken (fix first)

| # | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | Settings > Accounts | "Add Outlook" button is clipped at the left edge ("dd Outlook") |
| B2 | Settings > What Cluster learned | Row labels wrap onto two lines and collide with the value ("Sort / choices None") |
| B3 | Protect > Holding | Kai covers the "Send held mail to Spam" switch; on Clean up it covers the last row's button, on Stop mail the "Unsubscribe all" bar, on Settings the Theme menu |
| B4 | Organize > Sort now | 8 kinds plus 8 "keep in inbox" boxes wrap as one paragraph; boxes can't be matched to labels (see `2026-10-29-dashboards-and-option-labels.md`) |

## Per place

### Home
- Setup card ("Set Cluster up for you · Start · Not now") sits above the dashboard. T3: first-run job.
- Organize tile says "Let old mail leave on its own" with no number, unlike the other three tiles.
- Clean up tile: "191 of 356 ready to go", plus "Never opened" and "Past its date" rows that repeat Clean up's top two rows. Fine as entry points; they should open the place, not act.
- The Details panel is open by default and takes a third of the width on every place, including Settings and Protect, where it has nothing to do with the page. T3.

### Senders
- Best-structured place: Lists column, rows, card. Card for LinkedIn holds only "Mute all" plus addresses; single senders hold five actions (T1/T3, inventory).
- "Group companies" switch: only place with it. Clean up and Details don't group (N1). Should be the only behaviour, not a switch.
- Row subtitles mix "5 addresses · opens 21%" and "7 a month · noreply@medium.com".

### Clean up
- Top two rows: "Never opened · Mute" and "Past its date · Trash". Mute is Stop mail's verb (T1).
- "Most mail first 10 of 39" rows: Trash + ⋯ each. Good. ⋯ repeats Unsubscribe, Mute, Snooze (T1).
- Last row "Chase · 1 starred of 6 · excluded entirely" with a "Protect" button: unclear what it does.
- "Stop mail" is a sub-item under Clean up in the sidebar, the only nested item. It's a separate job (inventory), so either its own place or a section.

### Stop mail
- Starts with "Paid subscriptions & trials": Spotify, Netflix, Apple, then Amazon.com/GitHub/Google with "Seen". "Seen" isn't explained and these rows have no action. T4.
- Newsletters header packs "29 lists", "Unsubscribe all verified one-click", "Outcome: all", select-all, and "Problems first, then by volume" in two lines. T4, T6.
- Floating bar "22 stop in one click · Unsubscribe all" repeats the header's button. T6.

### Organize
- Five sections that move mail out of the inbox: Sort now, Archive after a while, Arrive on time, Tidy once, Your rules, plus Custom times and More tools collapsed. T2.
- Tidy once "Never opened · Move out" is the third home for never-opened mail (Clean up Mute, Home tile). "Newest 3 per sender · Move out" repeats Clean up's "Keep newest" Trim. T1.
- The guard line ("Starred mail and mail from people you write to never leave on a timer") floats between sections, far from the switches it qualifies.
- "More tools" opens to nothing. T5.

### Protect
- Three tiles act as tabs but read as numbers. T4.
- "Scams per week" chart is the largest tile and empty ("Turn on Hold likely scams under Holding to start"). The switch it points to is at the bottom of the page.
- Brands faked bars are tiny (1 each), the chart adds nothing over the High priority list above it.
- Holding switches, the place's main setting, are last and partly under Kai (B3).

### Settings
- Setup "Not set up yet · Change" duplicates the Home setup card.
- "Read my mail again · Rescan" duplicates the account menu's "Rescan mail". "Add Outlook" here, "Connect Outlook" in the account menu. T1.
- Safety net "Activity · Open" duplicates the account menu's "Activity". T1/T3.
- Show Kai switch is blue; every other switch in the app is gray/green style. One-off.
- "What Cluster learned" shows "None" three times in a broken layout (B2).
- Advanced holds only "Delete forever" (T3, inventory).

### Account menu
- Activity, Settings, Rescan mail, Connect Outlook. Two of four repeat Settings rows.

## Good, keep

Archive after a while and Holding (iOS rows: icon, name, one gray line, switch); Senders' Lists
column + card; Clean up's ranked list with one Trash each; High priority rows with brand logos
and one red Block; Settings group structure.
