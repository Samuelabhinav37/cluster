# Cluster audit (2026-11-19)

Where every part of Cluster stands against the goal, what's missing, and the order to finish it.
It builds on `goal-and-plan-2026-11-18.md` (steps like 3.7 refer to it) and doesn't replace it.

**The goal:** a Gmail user installs Cluster and signs in once. Every email lands in the right
label and the inbox holds only what needs them. Scams never reach them. They can see what's in
the mailbox and clear what they don't want in a few clicks. All of it runs on their own computer.

Status key: **Done** = built and tested. **Unproven** = built, never measured on a real mailbox.
**Missing** = not built.

## Summary

Most of the product is built, but almost none of it has been proven on a real mailbox.
About 1,500 tests pass on five mailboxes we wrote, scoring 99 to 100%. No real inbox has ever
been measured for a week. That one fact decides what comes next.

The biggest open risks:
1. **Google verification.** Without it Gmail sign-in is capped at 100 users. `gmail.modify` is a
   restricted scope, so it needs a yearly third-party security assessment. This takes months and
   hasn't started.
2. **Mail only leaves the inbox while Chrome is open.** Gmail filters only add labels. The
   one-hour move out of the inbox is done by the extension.
3. **Nothing has gone live.** 76 commits sit on a local branch. The weekly list refresh has
   never run, so the scam lists are 80 days old.
4. **Licences.** URLhaus is free only for not-for-profit use. StopForumSpam's terms are unclear.
   The Google favicon calls are a GDPR risk.

## Decisions (2026-11-19)

1. **Cluster never charges.** It's free and open source. So URLhaus and ThreatFox (abuse.ch, free
   for not-for-profit) stay, and the `URLHAUS_AUTH_KEY` secret is worth adding. Google Safe
   Browsing's free API (non-commercial only) becomes an option: its terms ask for lists under 30
   minutes old before warning, Google credit on its own warnings, and a "not perfect" notice.
   StopForumSpam staff say use inside a non-commercial product is fine, but we still republish
   their list, so keep asking for written OK.
2. **Keep the 1-hour wait for everything,** even with Chrome closed. Mail stays labelled in the
   inbox until Chrome opens. Say so plainly in setup and on the privacy page.
3. **Push** once the suite is green.
4. **Decision A:** keep the four groups on Overview (option 2 in `research/2026-11-19-overview-categories.md`).
