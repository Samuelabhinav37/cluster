Cluster

Cluster is removed.

Your mail is still in Gmail. Cluster's labels and filters are too, until you remove them.

  1. Remove its filters. In Gmail's filters, delete the ones that add a Cluster label. New mail then stays in your inbox.
  2. Remove its labels, if you like. In Gmail's sidebar, open a label's menu and choose Remove label. The mail stays in All Mail.
  3. End its Google access. In your Google account, open Cluster and remove the connection.

Coming back? Install Cluster again. Your labels are still there.
