Cluster

Cluster sorts and cleans up mail using only what your inbox already tells it — subjects, senders, dates, and starred/flagged status, never message bodies. Nothing is deleted without you confirming first, and everything runs from your own sign-in — no data leaves your browser except through provider APIs and optional features you explicitly enable.

Athena email security is available for this managed installation. Enabling the connection sends only minimized security evidence to your organization's Athena server. Mailbox cleanup stays local and suspicious mail is never auto-deleted.

Personalized cleanup suggestions

Cluster learns from changed unread patterns and your corrections, entirely on this device. It stores only bounded sender-level counts and ratios — never message IDs, subjects, or bodies. The displayed fit score explains why a suggestion appeared; it is not a probability. Starred or flagged senders are excluded, nothing moves until you confirm, and “Not useful” hides a suggestion for 30 days.

Suggested spam

Senders whose domain is on a public spam or throwaway-email list (disposable-email-domains, StopForumSpam) or the extension's own known-bad list. Usually safe to delete — but it's your call, and nothing moves to Trash until you confirm. Anything with a starred message is left out.

Ready to clean up

Messages that likely served their purpose — one-time codes, old shipping updates, and newsletters past their window, judged only by how long ago they arrived. Starred/flagged mail is always skipped. Nothing is deleted until you confirm.

By domain

Senders grouped by their base domain (personal addresses at gmail.com, yahoo.com, etc. are kept separate) and bucketed into categories. Deleting moves mail to Trash — starred messages are always skipped. "Select safe" only checks domains with zero starred/flagged mail.

Subscriptions

Every sender in this scan with an unsubscribe option, with problem outcomes shown first. Verified one-click requests fire straight away; others open the provider's page or an email. Requests are monitored locally and sorted into pending, quiet in the current scan, or still sending after a 14-day observation window; “quiet” is scan-limited evidence, not a claim that all delivery stopped. “Unsubscribe + clean” moves only newsletter-classified messages to Trash and keeps transactional, sensitive, starred, and ambiguous mail. “Read later” files the same safely classified newsletters into a reversible Cluster label instead of deleting them.

Possible impersonation

Senders that claim a well-known brand whose real domain doesn't match, fail authentication, use a lookalike domain, or appear on a known-bad domain list — common phishing patterns. Cluster scans recent Inbox mail separately from cleanup and uses provider-trusted authentication headers, but these are still risk signals rather than verified facts. Auto-quarantine acts only when the switch above is enabled; otherwise nothing here is moved or deleted. Ordered by risk.

    Rules

    Standing clean-up rules with priorities, exceptions, and optional stop-processing. Applied when you click "Apply" below and on every background sweep. Starred/flagged mail is always skipped; rules never permanently delete. Cluster previews overlapping rules before it acts.

    The on-device model receives only the instruction above—not email content—and can only draft constrained rule JSON. Cluster validates and previews it; nothing runs until you save and apply it. A deterministic parser is used when the model is unavailable.

    Screener

    Hold mail from senders you've never emailed until you decide about them. When on, the background sweep moves unknown senders' mail into a "Screener" label and out of your inbox. Your sent mail (who you've written to) is the automatic allow-list; nothing here is ever deleted.
