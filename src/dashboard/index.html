Connect Gmail to get started

Cluster finds mail you can clear out, sorts the rest, and flags senders that look like phishing. It runs entirely in your browser.

  • What it reads. Senders, subjects, dates, labels and the sender-authentication headers. Never message bodies.
  • What it never does. Send your mail anywhere, send email for you, or delete or move anything before you confirm.
  • Why Google asks to change your mail. So Cluster can label, archive, move to Trash and create filters when you tell it to.

Cluster is still in testing, so Google will show a "Google hasn't verified this app" screen first. Choose Continue to reach the permission screen.

Tip: pin Cluster from the puzzle-piece menu in Chrome's toolbar so it's one click away.

Loading your senders…

Cluster sorts and cleans up mail using only what your inbox already tells it — subjects, senders, dates, and starred/flagged status, never message bodies. Nothing is deleted without you confirming first, and everything runs from your own sign-in.

Athena email security is available for this managed installation. Enabling the connection sends only minimized security evidence to your organization's Athena server. Mailbox cleanup stays local and suspicious mail is never auto-deleted.

How long mail stays in your inbox

New mail gets its label straight away, even with Chrome closed. Moving it out of your inbox when its time is up happens every 15 minutes while Chrome is open.

Sort my inbox

Files your recent mail under labels like 🔑 One-time codes, 🧾 Receipts and 🛍 Shopping, based on what each email is or who sent it. Starred mail is left alone. You see a preview first, and nothing moves until you press Apply.

Which labels, and where the mail goes

For sender-based labels (Shopping, Travel, Finance and so on), Cluster adds a Gmail filter, so new mail gets its label the moment it arrives, even with Chrome closed. You can see them in Gmail under Settings, then Filters. One-time codes, receipts and shipping updates are sorted by Cluster's background check while Chrome is open.
