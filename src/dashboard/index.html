Connect Gmail to get started

Clean up, sort and protect your Gmail. It runs in your browser.

  • What it reads. Senders, subjects, dates, labels and the sender-authentication headers. Links only in mail that already looks like a scam.
  • What it never does. Send your mail anywhere, or send, delete or move anything before you confirm.
  • Why Google asks to change your mail. So Cluster can label, archive, move to Trash and create filters when you tell it to.

Google will say the app isn't verified yet. Choose Continue.
